SquidSpot is a land-based squid (eging) companion for Australia. Catalog pins are based on public catch and community reports. It is not legal advice, safety advice, or official fisheries advice. It is not approved by the Australian Government, AFMA, or any state or territory fisheries agency. A pin is not a guarantee of fish.

A map pin is not permission to fish and not permission to enter land or water. Access, seasons, rules and conditions change. Do not trespass. Private property, ports, leases, marine parks and closures can make a place unlawful to enter or to fish. You need the correct recreational fishing licence where one is required, and you must follow bag limits, size limits and gear rules.

Free includes a small set of public-report pins. Premium unlocks a denser catalog. Neither is a promise of fish or legal access.

Scores and conditions are a heuristic over offline demo data. They are not Bureau of Meteorology forecasts and not official tide tables. Jig tips are general suggestions only.

The subscription in this build is a demo entitlement stored on the device. It does not charge a card, does not start a Google Play subscription, and does not auto-renew through Google Play. Listed prices (A$15.99 per month and A$99.99 per year) are the intended prices if Play Billing is connected later. The yearly amount is about 48% less than twelve months at the monthly price. Until billing is live, those prices are not charged.

Nothing in these terms excludes a consumer guarantee under the Australian Consumer Law that cannot be excluded.

These terms should be reviewed by a lawyer, including the contracting entity and governing law, before production release.
